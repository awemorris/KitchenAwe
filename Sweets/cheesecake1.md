Cheesecake
==========

## What You Prepare

| Item           | Weight           |
|----------------|------------------|
| Cream Cheese   | 200g             |
| Fresh Cream    | 200ml            |
| Sugar          | 100g             |
| Sour Cream     | 200g             |
| Lemon Juice    | 3 tablespoon     |
| Whole Egg      | 2                |
| Cake Flour     | 15g              |

## Steps

1. Put `Creame Cheese`, `Fresh Cream`, and `Sugar`, into a bowl, then mix well.
2. Put `Sour Cream` into the bowl, then mix well.
3. Put `Whole Egg` into the bowl, then mix well.
4. Put `Cake Flour` into the bowl, then mix well.
5. Put `Lemon Juice` into the bowl, then mix well.
6. Taste it and adjust the `Sugar` and `Lemon Juice` as needed.
7. If it lacks richness, add a small amount of melted butter.
8. Crumple the parchment paper, dampen it with water, and line a 13-cm cake pan with it.
9. Pour the batter into the pan.
10. Bake in a 200°C oven for 40 minutes.
11. When the center wobbles slightly when you give it a gentle shake, it's done.
12. If the whole thing wobbles, it's not quite done yet, so bake it a little longer.
13. Let it chill in the refrigerator overnight. It's too watery to eat right after baking.
