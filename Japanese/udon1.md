Hot Kansai Udon Soup
====================

## What You Prepare

A:
| Item                              | Weight           |
|-----------------------------------|------------------|
| Konbu Soup Stock (Liquid)         | 1 tablespoon     |
| Shiro-Dashi Soup Stock (Liquid)   | 1 tablespoon     |
| Soy Sauce (Usukuchi)              | 1 tablespoon     |
| Sake (Japanese Cooking Wine)      | 1 tablespoon     |
| Mirin                             | 1 tablespoon     |
| Sugar                             | 0.5 tablespoon   |
| Water                             | 300 ml           |

B: Optional

- Green Onion Slice
- Raw Egg
- White Sesami

## Steps

1. Put everything of "A" in a pan and get it boiled.
2. Put the soup in a bowl.
3. Put your noodle in a bowl, that's a complete Soba!
4. Put "B" on Soba if you like.
